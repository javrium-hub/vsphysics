Meet your teacher

Physics that makes sense the first time you hear it.

Vivek Shrivastava has spent more than eleven years teaching one subject: physics. His students are board toppers-in-the-making from Indian classrooms, JEE and NEET aspirants across the country, and AP students in schools around the world.

His classes start with what is actually happening, a ball, a field, a wave, and only then bring in the maths. That is why students who once memorised formulas start deriving them.
